Before the first visit

Three tiny tips for parents.

Keep it light

Call it a visit to a friendly dentist. Skip words like "shot" or "drill". The team uses kind words instead.

Let them explore

Children over 3 are welcome to come back with the team on their own. It builds trust fast.

Tears are okay

Nerves are normal. The team is used to anxious kids and goes at your child's pace.

Tooth bump? Ouch.

For a knocked tooth or sudden pain, call right away. For a serious injury, go to the ER.
